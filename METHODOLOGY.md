# ATLAS methodology

Production shocks are applied to observed baseline production. Trade exposure uses observed bilateral wheat trade values only; missing values are not converted to zero. Supplier shares are value-weighted by importer and HHI is the sum of squared supplier shares.

Temporal propagation is a deterministic month-level traversal using graph delay metadata. Intervention output is a modeled proportional offset, not a forecast of actual substitution. Price pressure and confidence are transparent provisional coefficients documented in the scenario response assumptions.

Every result is classified as observed, derived, modeled, provisional, or unavailable. Unsupported climate, logistics, input, and energy scenarios return structured unavailable responses until validated observations and coefficients are supplied.
